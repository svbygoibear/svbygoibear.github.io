## Welcome

This is the first post on my technical blog. I'll be writing about things I find interesting in the world of software — tools, patterns, war stories, and the occasional deep dive.

## What to expect

Posts here will generally fall into a few categories:

- **Engineering notes** — lessons from real projects, things that didn't work, things that did
- **Tool & library writeups** — hands-on experience with tech worth knowing about
- **Thinking out loud** — early-stage ideas and opinions

## Writing workflow

Every post lives as a Markdown file in the repository. When a post is ready to go live, I remove the `draft: true` flag and merge the PR — GitHub Actions takes care of the rest.

If you prefer feeds, the blog is available via [RSS](/rss.xml).
