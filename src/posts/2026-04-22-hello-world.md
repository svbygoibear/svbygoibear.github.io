## Welcome

This is the first post on my technical blog. I'll be writing about things I find interesting in the world of software — tools, patterns, war stories, and the occasional deep dive.

## What to expect

Posts here will generally fall into a few categories:

- **Engineering notes** — lessons from real projects, things that didn't work, things that did
- **Tool & library writeups** — hands-on experience with tech worth knowing about
- **Thinking out loud** — early-stage ideas and opinions

## Community and giving back

Beyond the code, one of my biggest motivations is building and strengthening the technical community around me. I'm involved with [DevOps Lisbon](https://www.meetup.com/devops-lisbon/) and genuinely believe that knowledge shared openly is knowledge that compounds — for everyone.

Going forward I plan to:

- **Speak at conferences and meetups** — sharing real experiences from the field, not just polished success stories
- **Connect with community members** — whether that's at events, online, or through this blog
- **Contribute back** — through talks, open discussions, and helping others navigate the same problems I've stumbled through

If you're involved in the DevOps or platform engineering space in Lisbon (or beyond), I'd love to connect.

## Writing workflow

Every post lives as a Markdown file in the repository. When a post is ready to go live, I remove the `draft: true` flag and merge the PR — GitHub Actions takes care of the rest.

If you prefer feeds, the blog is available via [RSS](/rss.xml).
